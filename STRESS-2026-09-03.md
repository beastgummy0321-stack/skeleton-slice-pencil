## What changed (v5.7.0)

- `hooks/ticket-fields.mjs`: fails closed on a bad payload; every general-purpose dispatch names its ticket (under `docs/issues/` only) or its post prefix. One old expectation flipped in the test ("no ticket named: passes through" asserted the fail-open design), three added.
- `rules/workflow.md`: Premise check gains the mandatory final-form option; the final form is asked, never inferred; new section "A boundary escalation is not a menu"; posts are named in dispatch prompts; the volume line names files above 80% of the line cap.
- `rules/container-contract.md`: §7 shape inside the module (handler files carry no business branch, money never lives in routes; a line cap is an alarm); Python's gate is `import-linter`.
- `skills/skeleton`: "what does it finally become" enters the question whitelist and is tree question 2, mandatory, recommendation leaning large.
- `skills/slice`: §1 runs `git status` first and asks keep/discard on a dirty tree.

## Withdrawn

The 2026-09-03 review's proposal 1 — take ponytail out of always-on — is withdrawn on T1a/T1b evidence. The user's position (architecture settled first, ponytail writes less inside it) is what the measurement shows.
